Quick Action Wheel PRD

Full PRD: a hold-to-open quick wheel on the timeframe button of Sahi's mobile Option Scalper. Use this to design the wheel in Figma and to build a clickable prototype (Appendix A).

Product type: Mobile app · Depth: Full PRD · Version: v1.2 · Date: 8 Nov 2026 · Status: Draft

Owner: Suhas (concept author) · Reviewers / sign-off: Open item

Scope note: a personal concept prepared for a Sahi design interview, not an official Sahi project. The existing Option Scalper screen and its menus are read from screenshots taken in Nov 2026 (R1 to R3). Anything read off a screenshot is marked Assumption.

Changes: v1.0 replaces the first concept (a wheel icon dragged out of the chart divider, R4) with a hold on the existing timeframe button, because the divider already resizes the charts and the drag competed with it. v1.1 replaces the two rings with one larger ring. v1.2 sets the ring to 7 slots: 2 timeframes, Trend Line, Fibonacci Retracement, VWAP, EMA and Clean view.

For Claude Code: build the prototype described in Appendix A. Sections 4 to 10 define the behaviour; where Appendix A gives numbers, use them as starting values.

1. Overview

  • Problem / why: On the mobile Option Scalper, every chart change goes through the timeframe button's sheet. Turning on a favourite indicator or picking a drawing tool takes 3 or more taps plus a search or scroll, and keeping the drawing toolbar open takes chart width a phone can't spare. Scalpers flip between 1m, 5m and 15m to read the micro and the bigger trend mid-trade, when seconds matter. Traders on public forums ask for the same things: fewer steps on mobile charts and higher-timeframe context without switching screens (problem bank P157, P159, P160).
  • Users / roles: Trader on Sahi's Option Scalper, phone in portrait (option buyers and intraday scalpers).
  • Main jobs: switch a chart's timeframe; turn a favourite indicator on or off; pick a favourite drawing tool; clear the chart to read price.
  • Entry points: the timeframe button ("5m" with a menu icon) at the top right of the index chart and of the option chart, in S1 Option Scalper.
  • In scope: the hold-to-open wheel M1 and its feedback; teaching (N1, tip row in B1); slot customisation (S2, B2, M2); result feedback (N2, N3). Out of scope / owned elsewhere: order placement; strike and quantity changes (tapping the strike already opens the option chain); the indicator and drawing engines; landscape; tablet; web and desktop scalper.
  • Platforms and frames: iOS and Android, portrait. Frames 390×844 (iOS) and 360×800 (Android). The prototype uses 390×844.

1.3 Constraints, dependencies and risks

Risk Impact Mitigation or owner
The hold is a hidden gesture Few traders find it N1 coachmark and the tip row in B1; track discovery
A trader who means to tap holds a little too long The wheel opens unexpectedly Hold threshold (Assumption: 300 ms); releasing on nothing cancels; track accidental opens and tune
Small saving for timeframes Today a timeframe change is already 2 taps Lead with indicators, drawing tools and clean view, where the saving is 3 or more taps
Reach to the top chart's button It sits near the top of the phone, hard to reach one-handed on large phones Test on a 6.7-inch phone; fallback is an Open item
The wheel covers the latest candles while held The trader loses sight of price briefly It shows only while the finger is down; slots are translucent (Assumption); data keeps updating underneath
Left-handed use A left thumb comes from the bottom left and covers the down-left fan Open item
Linked charts A timeframe picked on one chart also changes the other Link mark on timeframe slots; N2 says "Both charts"
Clean view hides something critical The trader forgets an open position or stop BR-07: clean view never hides positions, orders or SL/TP
Dependency: indicator settings The VWAP and EMA slots use the trader's saved indicator settings Assumption: saved settings are available on mobile; owner: Sahi charts team

Gesture conflicts Edge swipe, price-axis drag or the divider could compete The button is inset from the screen edge and off the price axis; the divider is untouched; verify on device

NFR-03 Touch targets Each slot is at least 48 dp wide along the arc at the ring's outer edge; because selection is by angle, the target keeps widening as the finger slides further out M1

4. User Flows

Flow 1: Switch the option chart to 1m

Actor: Trader · Starts: S1 Option Scalper, option chart on 5m · Ends: S1, option chart on 1m

  • Trader presses and holds the "5m" button on the option chart in S1 Option Scalper.
  • System scales the button down slightly while the finger is down.
  • At the hold threshold, system plays a light haptic, outlines the option chart panel and opens M1 Quick Action Wheel anchored on the button.
  • Trader slides left onto the "1m" slot at the top of the ring.
  • System highlights the slot, shows the hover label "Timeframe 1m", plays a selection haptic and previews "1m" on the button.
  • Trader releases on the slot.
  • System closes M1, switches the option chart to 1m and updates the button to "1m". Trader stays on S1.
  • If the charts are linked, system also switches the index chart and shows N2 "Both charts set to 1m".
  • If 1m is already the current timeframe, the slot is Active and releasing on it changes nothing.
  • If the phone is offline, timeframe slots are Unavailable ("Offline") and releasing on them changes nothing.
  • If the 1m data fails to load, system keeps the chart on 5m and shows N2 "Couldn't load 1m. Try again." (Assumption)

Flow 2: Turn on a favourite indicator on the index chart

Actor: Trader · Starts: S1, VWAP off on the index chart · Ends: S1, VWAP on

  • Trader holds the "5m" button on the index chart in S1.
  • System opens M1 for the index chart.
  • Trader slides down and to the left onto "VWAP" in the ring.
  • Trader releases.
  • System closes M1, adds VWAP to the index chart with the trader's saved settings, and shows N2 "VWAP on".
  • If VWAP was already on, system removes it and shows N2 "VWAP off".
  • If clean view was on for that chart, system turns clean view off first (BR-08).
  • If the indicator slot is empty, releasing on "+ Add" opens S2 Customise Quick Wheel at the Indicators section.

Flow 3: Pick a drawing tool and draw a trend line

Actor: Trader · Starts: S1 · Ends: S1, trend line drawn on the option chart

  • Trader holds the "5m" button on the option chart in S1.
  • System opens M1 for the option chart.
  • Trader slides onto "Trend" in the ring and releases.
  • System closes M1, arms the Trend Line tool on the option chart and shows N3 "Tap two points on the chart to draw a Trend Line".
  • Trader taps the first point, then the second point on the option chart.
  • System draws the line between the two points and closes N3. Trader stays on S1.
  • If trader taps Cancel on N3, system disarms the tool and closes N3.
  • If trader holds a timeframe button again before finishing, system disarms the tool, closes N3 and opens M1.
  • If clean view was on for that chart, system turns clean view off first (BR-08).

Flow 4: Cancel, or an accidental open

Actor: Trader · Starts: S1 · Ends: S1, nothing changed

  • Trader holds a timeframe button and M1 opens.
  • Trader decides not to change anything and slides back onto the button (dead zone) or out of the wheel.
  • System clears the highlight and the button shows the current timeframe again.
  • Trader releases.
  • System closes M1 with no change and no success haptic. Trader stays on S1.
  • If the trader lifts before the hold threshold without moving, system treats it as a tap and opens B1 Chart Menu.
  • If the trader moves beyond the tap tolerance before the threshold, nothing opens.
  • If a second finger touches the screen, the app goes to the background, a call comes in or the screen locks while M1 is open, system closes M1 with no change.

Flow 5: Learn the hold

Actor: Trader · Starts: first S1 visit with the feature on · Ends: S1, coachmark dismissed

  • Trader opens S1 Option Scalper.
  • System shows N1 coachmark on the index chart's timeframe button: "New: hold 5m for quick switches".
  • Trader taps "Got it", or holds the button.
  • System dismisses N1 for good.
  • Later, trader taps a timeframe button.
  • System opens B1 Chart Menu with the tip row "Tip: hold 5m to open the quick wheel" at the top.
  • If the trader has applied 3 items with the wheel, or dismissed the tip, system stops showing the tip row (BR-16).

Flow 6: Customise the slots

Actor: Trader · Starts: S1 · Ends: S2 with a slot changed

  • Trader taps a timeframe button and B1 Chart Menu opens.
  • Trader taps "Customise" in the tip row, or "Quick wheel" in the Settings tab.
  • System opens S2 Customise Quick Wheel with a preview of the current slots.
  • Trader taps "Slot 2" under Timeframes.
  • System opens B2 Slot Picker with the timeframe chips.
  • Trader taps "15m".
  • System closes B2, saves the change, sorts the timeframes shortest to longest and updates the preview on S2.
  • Trader taps Back and returns to B1.
  • If the trader taps "Reset to defaults", system opens M2 Reset Quick Wheel.
  • If saving fails, system keeps the previous value and shows N2 "Couldn't save. Try again."

Workflow

  • Trader watches both charts, with the order panel open or closed.
  • Trader taps a timeframe button for the full menu (B1), or holds it for the wheel (M1).
  • After M1 applies an item, the chart updates in place and the trader stays on S1.
  • Clean view and an armed drawing tool show their own markers (Clean view chip, N3), so the trader always knows the chart's mode.

M1 Quick Action Wheel

Type: Overlay, radial menu (open only while the finger stays down) · Platform: iOS and Android, portrait · Opened from: S1, hold on either chart's timeframe button · Roles: Trader

Purpose: Apply a timeframe, a favourite indicator, a favourite drawing tool or clean view to the held chart with one press, slide and release.

Layout:

  1. Anchor: the held timeframe button at the top right of its chart panel. The wheel's centre is the button's centre. The button stays visible and shows the current timeframe, or the hovered one as a preview.
  2. Shape: a quarter circle that fans from the button's left (9 o'clock) round to straight below it (6 o'clock), into the chart and away from a right thumb. Nothing opens above or to the right of the button, where the chart header, price axis and screen edge are.
  3. Dead zone: a small circle around the button. Releasing there cancels.
  4. One ring of 7 equal slots, from 9 o'clock to 6 o'clock, in four groups with a small gap between groups: Timeframe 1, Timeframe 2 · Trend Line, Fibonacci Retracement · VWAP, EMA · Clean view. The ring is as large as the chart panel allows, so each slot stays wide enough for a thumb.
  5. Defaults: 1m and 5m (Assumption) · Trend Line, Fibonacci Retracement · VWAP, EMA (with the trader's saved settings) · Clean view.
  6. Hover label: the hovered item's full name in a pill, in one fixed spot just above the wheel's top edge and left of the button (over the chart header), so it never sits under the thumb or over a slot.
  7. The rest of the chart stays visible and live. Slot backgrounds are translucent dark (Assumption) so candles show through.

Workflow

  • Trader holds the timeframe button; at the threshold the wheel opens for that chart.

10. Business Rules

  • BR-01: The wheel holds view actions only: timeframe, clean view, indicators and drawing tools. It never contains buy, sell, exit, strike, quantity or any order action.
  • BR-02: Every action applies to the chart whose button was held. Exception: when the charts are linked, a timeframe change applies to both (Assumption on what the Link icon does).
  • BR-03: Slot positions never change while the trader uses the wheel. Unavailable slots are greyed out and empty slots show "+ Add"; nothing is removed or reordered except through S2.
  • BR-04: Both charts use the same slot layout.
  • BR-05: There are exactly 2 timeframe slots. They are unique, required and always sorted shortest to longest. Clean view is always slot 7, the last slot at 6 o'clock.
  • BR-06: Slots 3 to 4 hold up to 2 drawing tools (default Trend Line and Fibonacci Retracement) and slots 5 to 6 hold up to 2 indicators (default VWAP and EMA). Each is unique and each slot may be empty.
  • BR-07: Clean view hides indicators and drawings on that chart. It never hides candles, axes, the last price, the open position line, pending orders or SL/TP pills.
  • BR-08: Turning on an indicator or arming a drawing tool while clean view is on turns clean view off for that chart first (Assumption).
  • BR-09: Lifting before the hold threshold without moving is a tap and opens B1. Moving beyond the tap tolerance before the threshold opens nothing. Assumption: threshold 300 ms and tolerance 10 dp, tuned in testing.
  • BR-10: Releasing anywhere other than an available, non-Active slot changes nothing.
  • BR-11: Only one wheel can be open at a time. A second touch closes it with no change.
  • BR-12: The wheel never pauses or delays live prices, P&L or order updates.
  • BR-13: Indicators turned on from the wheel use the trader's saved settings for that indicator; the wheel has no indicator settings of its own.
  • BR-14: Turning clean view off restores exactly what was showing before it was turned on.
  • BR-15: Whether clean view stays on after leaving S1 or restarting the app is an Open item.
  • BR-16: The tip row in B1 stops showing after the trader dismisses it or applies 3 items with the wheel (Assumption).
  • BR-17: Wheel slots are saved per user. Whether they sync across the trader's devices is an Open item.

Appendix A. Prototype build notes (for Claude Code)

These are starting values for a clickable prototype, not product decisions. Where they differ from Sections 4 to 10, Sections 4 to 10 win.

A.1 Stack and frame

  • One self-contained HTML file: HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Charts drawn on canvas; the wheel drawn in SVG. No build step, no external libraries.
  • A 390×844 phone frame centred on desktop; full screen when opened on a phone. Dark theme matching R1.
  • Use Pointer Events so mouse and touch both work. Set touch-action: none on the timeframe buttons and the wheel layer.
  • Outside the phone frame, on desktop only, show a small dev panel (A.8).

A.2 Screen and mock data

  • Build S1 as in Figure 2: top bar, index chart panel, divider pill, option chart panel, order panel (Buy at Mkt, quantity stepper "65 · 1 lot", Sell at Mkt). The order panel can stay visible.
  • Index: random-walk 1-minute candles around 25,030 for one session (about 375 bars). Option "09 Nov 25000 Call": premium around 160 that moves with about half the index move plus noise.
  • Tick every 1 second: update the last candle's close, high and low; roll to a new candle every 60 ticks.
  • Support intervals 1m, 2m, 3m, 5m, 10m, 15m, 25m, 30m by aggregating 1-minute bars. Second and Hour intervals can show in the pickers but are disabled with "Not in prototype".
  • Mock indicators: VWAP (cumulative) and EMA 20, drawn as lines. Defaults in the wheel: VWAP, EMA 20.
  • On the option chart, show an open position line "Long 65 @ 140.00" with an SL pill at 120 and a TP pill at 180, to prove clean view keeps them (BR-07).
  • Link icon on the index chart header toggles linked timeframes. Default: off.

A.3 Hold and gesture logic

  • On pointerdown on a timeframe button: capture the pointer, add the Pressed state, start a hold timer (300 ms).
  • If the pointer moves more than 10 px before the timer fires: cancel; nothing opens.
  • If the pointer lifts before the timer and within 10 px: it's a tap; open the B1 mock.
  • When the timer fires: open the wheel for that chart, add the chart target outline, call navigator.vibrate?.(10) where supported.
  • On every pointermove while open: hit-test (A.4); when the hovered slot changes, update the highlight, hover label and button preview, and call navigator.vibrate?.(5).
  • On pointerup: apply the hovered slot if it's available and not Active; otherwise cancel. Close the wheel.
  • On pointercancel, a second pointerdown, visibilitychange to hidden, or window blur: close with no change.
  • Log every open, select and cancel to the dev panel with the event names in M1 Notes and timings in ms.

A.4 Wheel geometry

  • Anchor: the centre of the held timeframe button (cx, cy).
  • For a pointer at (x, y): dx = x − cx, dy = y − cy (screen y grows downward). r = hypot(dx, dy). θ = atan2(dy, dx) in degrees, normalised to 0 to 360, where 0 is right, 90 is down and 180 is left.
  • The wheel covers θ from 90 (straight down) to 180 (straight left). If θ is between 80 and 90 or between 180 and 190, clamp to the nearest edge slot. Any other angle counts as outside.
  • Hit test: r < 40 is the dead zone. Beyond that, selection is by angle only, at any distance (overshoot allowed anywhere on screen).
  • Ring: one ring drawn from r = 120 to R, where R = min(280, distance from the button centre to the held panel's bottom edge − 8, distance to its left edge − 8). At 390×844, R = 280, which keeps each slot about 63 px wide along the arc at the outer edge and about 50 px at the labels (NFR-03). Labels sit at r = 0.8 × R.
  • 7 slots of 90/7 (about 12.86°) each, from 180 (left) to 90 (down). Slot i covers 180 − i × 90/7 to 180 − (i − 1) × 90/7: slot 1 (1m) about 167.1 to 180; slot 2 (5m) 154.3 to 167.1; slot 3 (Trend Line) 141.4 to 154.3; slot 4 (Fibonacci Retracement) 128.6 to 141.4; slot 5 (VWAP) 115.7 to 128.6; slot 6 (EMA) 102.9 to 115.7; slot 7 (Clean view) 90 to 102.9.
  • Draw each slot as an SVG annular sector with a 2 px gap inside a group and a 6 px gap between groups (after slots 2, 4 and 6). Default fill: dark grey at about 85% opacity. Hovered: accent fill and label scaled to 1.15. Active: outline. On: small dot. Unavailable: 40% opacity. Empty: "+ Add".
  • Hover label: a pill centred at (cx − 150, cy − 12), just above the wheel's top edge, so it never covers a slot or sits under the thumb; clamp it inside the phone frame.
  • Check: at 390×844 the top chart's button sits at about (342, 85) and the bottom chart's at about (342, 415); a 280 px ring reaches about y = 365 and y = 695, inside both chart panels.

A.5 What each item does in the prototype

  • Timeframe: re-aggregate candles for that chart and update the button label. If linked, do both charts and show N2.
  • Clean view: hide VWAP, EMA 20 and drawings on that chart, show the Clean view chip, keep the position, SL and TP lines. Turning it off restores what was showing.
  • Indicator: toggle the line on that chart and show N2.
  • Trend Line: show N3; two taps place a line between two points.
  • Fibonacci Retracement: show N3; two taps place levels at 0, 23.6, 38.2, 50, 61.8 and 100%.
  • "+ Add": open the S2 mock at that section.

A.6 Other surfaces in the prototype

  • N1: show on first load (flag in localStorage, wrapped in try/catch); "Got it" or a hold dismisses it.
  • B1: a bottom sheet with the tip row, the tab row (Indicator and Drawing tabs can show a simple "Existing sheet" placeholder) and the timeframe chips. Tapping a chip switches the timeframe and closes the sheet.
  • S2, B2, M2: working screens as specified; save slot choices to localStorage (try/catch) and update the wheel immediately.
  • N2: toast above the order panel for 2 seconds.

A.7 Motion and feedback

  • Wheel open: 120 ms fade and scale from 0.9 to 1. Close: 80 ms fade. With prefers-reduced-motion, fade only.
  • Pressed button: scale 0.94.
  • Haptics through navigator.vibrate work on Android Chrome only; on iOS the prototype relies on the visual feedback.

A.8 Dev panel (desktop only, outside the phone frame)

  • Toggle "Show hit zones": draws the dead zone, the ring and the slot angle lines over the chart.
  • Slider "Hold threshold": 200 to 500 ms, default 300.
  • Toggle "Linked charts" and "Offline" (Offline makes timeframe slots Unavailable).
  • Button "Reset demo": clears localStorage flags and slot settings.
  • Event log: the last 20 analytics events with timings.

A.9 Acceptance checklist

  • A tap on either timeframe button opens B1; a hold opens the wheel on that chart only.
  • A fast flick (release under 200 ms after the wheel opens) on any slot applies it.
  • Releasing in the dead zone, outside the quarter circle, on the Active timeframe or on an Unavailable slot changes nothing.
  • With linked charts, a timeframe change updates both charts and both buttons, and shows N2.
  • Clean view hides indicators and drawings but keeps the position, SL and TP lines, and turning it off restores them.
  • A second finger, switching tabs or blurring the window while the wheel is open closes it with no change.
  • Candles keep ticking while the wheel is open.
  • Works with mouse on desktop and touch on a phone.
