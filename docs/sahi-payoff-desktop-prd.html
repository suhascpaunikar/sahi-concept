Example values come from the research model (Nifty 25,000, IV 12%, range at IV 10% to 15%) and are illustrative, not market data.

Changes in v1.1: the P key toggle is replaced by hold-to-peek. Holding ⌥ Option (Mac) or Alt (Windows) shows Payoff and letting go hides it; the Payoff button on the toolbar still keeps it on until it is clicked again or Esc is pressed (BR-23). Copy for the button, its tooltip, N1 and the Payoff header follows.

Changes in v1.2: Payoff opens on the view the trader already has. It no longer fits the charts to an expiry; the future zone starts in the space right of Now, and the trader drags, zooms or picks a horizon chip to look further ahead (BR-24). The index chart and the option chart keep their own timeframe, scroll and zoom, e.g. Nifty on 3m and the option on 5m (BR-25). The Payoff button drops its key hint; its tooltip and N1 still teach the key. In the Payoff table, a leg squared off before the point is greyed out and its P&L still counts in the total (BR-12).

Changes in v1.3: both charts drag left into history and right into empty space up to 3:30 pm on the next trading day, with Payoff on or off (BR-27). Past the axis end, the chart is striped and disabled: no shading, no point, no pin (BR-26). Payoff no longer draws the expected-move cone and has no Expected move toggle (FR-14 removed). The hover readout leads with the P&L: the time, then a larger P&L, then the range; the index level moves to the price-axis label and the If Nifty stays at line leaves the readout (it stays in the table, the option chart and D1).

Changes in v1.4: the chart toolbar's timeframe and range controls are specified (they are Sahi's existing controls; Payoff doesn't change them). A new timeframe keeps the candle width, so the window grows or shrinks with the candle size; candles start at 9:15 am each session (BR-25). The prototype now runs on real 1-minute data from Zerodha Kite for NIFTY 50 and the NIFTY 19 Oct 22500 Call and Put, up to 12:59 pm on Wed 14 Oct; later times are simulated and other contracts are modelled from the real index. The PRD's worked examples still use the research model (Nifty 25,000).

Changes in v1.7: the option chart's point label becomes a card in the same style as the index chart's readout, leading with the P&L in large type, then its range and the premium (BR-30).

Changes in v1.6: the charts open less crowded, at about 11 px per candle, and zoom smoothly with a trackpad pinch, the mouse wheel or a touch-screen pinch, keeping the minute under the pointer in place; a sideways trackpad swipe pans (BR-29).

Changes in v1.5: the crosshair is mirrored between the two charts (BR-28). Hovering the Nifty chart draws a second crosshair on the option chart at the same minute, whatever either timeframe, at the option price that matches the hovered Nifty level; hovering the option chart does the same on Nifty. The other chart scrolls to keep that minute in view. Crosshair time labels show the exact minute.

1. Overview

  • Problem / why: 87.7% of individual F&O traders lost money in FY26, about 97% mainly buy options, and 59% of index option turnover is in contracts expiring that same day (SEBI). Traders read levels on the Nifty chart, but their P&L lives in premium, and delta, time decay and IV move premium in ways they can't see. On expiry day a 50-point Nifty rise by the close can still leave an at-the-money call below what was paid.
  • Users / roles: One role, a trader with F&O enabled using the Scalper on desktop. Three personas: expiry-day option buyer, option seller, positional option buyer (1.2).
  • Main jobs: see where each position breaks even now and at every future time; see P&L for any index level at any time up to expiry; for multi-leg books, see each leg and the total; in the Later release, turn a chosen point into a target, a stop or an exit time.
  • Entry points: the Payoff button on the index chart toolbar in the Scalper split view, which keeps Payoff on, and holding ⌥ Option (Mac) or Alt (Windows) anywhere in the Scalper, which shows Payoff only while the key is held (hold-to-peek).
  • In scope: index options and index futures on the index loaded in the left chart (Nifty, Bank Nifty, Sensex and any other index the Scalper supports); one or several positions; weekly and monthly expiries; intraday and carry-forward positions; a pre-trade view when nothing is held. · Out of scope / owned elsewhere: mobile (separate PRD); sign-in, account and F&O activation (existing Sahi flows); stock options; the option seller terminal's strategy payoff (owned by that terminal); manual IV sliders; order rules for targets, stops and time exits (owned by Orders).
  • Platforms and frames: Sahi web and the Sahi Desktop app share one layout. Frames: 1440 × 900 primary, 1280 × 800 minimum, 1920 × 1080 large.

4. User Flows

Flow 1: Check one position before deciding to hold or exit

Actor
Trader (expiry-day option buyer)
Starts
S1 at 10:15 on Mon 19 Oct, holding Buy 65 NIFTY 19 OCT 25000 CE at ₹69.10, carry-forward
Ends
S1
  • Trader rests the pointer on the index chart in S1 Scalper split view and holds ⌥ Option (Mac) or Alt (Windows).
  • System opens S2 Payoff mode: one position, and switches the option chart to NIFTY 19 OCT 25000 CE if another contract was showing. Both charts keep the view, timeframe and zoom the trader had (BR-24, BR-25).
  • System draws the breakeven line and the profit shading in the space right of Now, with no horizon chip picked; the breakeven label gives the level at the right edge of the view.
  • Trader clicks the Today chip, or drags the index chart left, to bring the rest of today into view.
  • System shows the future zone to 3:30 pm, the Weekly expiry · Mon 19 Oct marker and the label Breakeven 25,069 at expiry.
  • Trader hovers the index chart at 25,050 and 11:15.
  • System shows the readout: 11:15 am, Mon 19 Oct, then P&L +₹1,385 in large type, then Range +₹751 to +₹2,355; the price axis shows 25,050.
  • System shows on the option chart a marker at the hovered time and, beside it, a card in the same style as the readout: 11:15 am, Mon 19 Oct, then P&L +₹1,385 in large type, Range +₹751 to +₹2,355 and Premium ₹90.40, with the premium range ₹80.65 to ₹105.33 drawn as a bar on the marker; the Paid ₹69.10 line and the flat-case line stay on the chart.
  • Trader moves the pointer to 3:30 pm at 25,050.
  • System shows P&L -₹1,241: a 50-point rise by the close still loses, because the time value has gone.
  • If the position is intraday (the Scalper default), system ends the future zone at 3:20 pm with the Auto square-off · 3:20 pm marker instead of the expiry marker, and the readout at 25,050 and 3:20 pm shows P&L -₹1,197.
  • Trader drags the index chart further right, past 3:30 pm: system shows the rest of the chart up to 3:30 pm tomorrow striped and labelled After expiry; nothing there can be hovered.
  • If the range grows wider than the premium in the afternoon, system shows Time value left instead of the range.
  • If prices go stale, system greys the values and shows N2 Pricing status banner, Stale variant.
  • Trader lets go of the key.
  • System closes Payoff and returns to S1, restoring the option contract shown before. Had the trader clicked Payoff instead of holding the key, Payoff would stay on until they click it again or press Esc.

Actor
Trader
Starts

Workflow

  • Trader clicks a point in the future zone on S2 or S3, or presses Enter at the focused point.
  • System places a pin and opens D1 with the values at that point.
  • Trader drags the pin or clicks elsewhere to adjust it; values and button prices update.
  • Trader picks Set target, Set stop, Exit at or Exit all at, and system opens M1.
  • Trader closes the card or presses Esc to remove the pin and return to S2 or S3.

Notes

  • Responsive / platform: the card has a fixed width and flips to the left of the pin near the right edge, and above it near the bottom.
  • Analytics: payoff_pin_place, payoff_pin_move, payoff_pin_action_click with the action, payoff_pin_close.
  • Ownership / dependencies: order rules belong to Orders (BR-20 to BR-22); Payoff only prefills.
  • Accessibility: focus moves into the card when it opens; Tab moves through the buttons; Esc closes it and returns focus to the chart at the pin.

10. Business Rules

IDRuleSurfaces
BR-01Payoff is available when the left chart shows an index with listed options. It includes every open option and futures position on that index, across all expiries and both products (intraday and carry-forward). Positions on other underlyings are left out and counted in the Other-charts chipS1, S2, S3
BR-02A position is one contract with non-zero open quantity. Fills in the same contract merge at the average price. A partly exited position counts at its open quantity, and the exited part's P&L is realised. Zero or one position opens S2; two or more open S3. Open item: whether a single futures position uses S2 or S3 (proposal: S3 with a one-row table, because the right chart shows options)S2, S3
BR-03The future zone ends at 3:30 pm on the latest expiry date among open positions on the charted index. When every open position is intraday, it ends at today's auto square-off. In a book with both intraday and carry-forward positions, the latest carry-forward expiry sets the end. Pre-trade, it ends at the expiry of the contract on the option chartS2, S3
BR-04Intraday Scalper positions are squared off at 3:20 pm. Open item: Sahi's FAQs give both 3:20 pm and 3:15 pm; the marker and the axis end use the confirmed timeS2, S3, D1
BR-05Expiry dates come from the exchange contract list, never from a weekday rule. Today, Nifty weeklies expire on Tuesday and the monthly on the last Tuesday, Sensex weeklies on Thursday, and Bank Nifty has monthly contracts only. An expiry that falls on a trading holiday moves to the previous trading day, e.g. Tue 20 Oct 2026 to Mon 19 Oct (Dussehra) and Tue 24 Nov 2026 to Mon 23 Nov (Guru Nanak Jayanti)S2, S3
BR-06There is one expiry marker per distinct expiry date held: Weekly expiry · {date}, Monthly expiry · {date}, or Weekly and monthly expiry · {date} in the month-end week, when they are one contract. A holiday shift adds moved from {original date}S2, S3
BR-07A breakeven line joins, at each moment in the future zone, the index levels where the total open P&L of the included positions is zero. It leaves out today's realised P&L, and charges (Assumption, as most payoff tools do). Profit shading covers levels where total open P&L is above zero. A buyer and a seller of the same contract see the same line, with the shading flippedS2, S3
BR-08Each option leg is valued at the hovered level and time from its live price and implied volatility, with IV held at its current level. Futures legs move point for point with the index. Values use the full open quantity, with lot sizes from the contract list (Nifty: 65). Open item: pricing model (the research used Black-Scholes on a trading-time clock, priced off index futures or a synthetic forward)S2, S3, D1
BR-09The range shows P&L if IV moves up or down from its current level. Open item: method and width. The research examples use IV 10% and 15% around a current 12%; the proposal is to set the width from past expiry days so that about 80% of outcomes land insideS2, S3, D1
BR-10On a contract's expiry day, when the range is wider than the premium at the point, the range is replaced by Too uncertain to price. Time value left: ₹X. Worth at expiry at this level: ₹YS2, S3, D1
BR-11At any point past a leg's expiry, the leg shows Expired and is valued at its intrinsic value at the hovered level (decided 13 Oct). After 3:30 pm on expiry day, positions that expired that day show their settled P&L, then drop off at the next sessionS2, S3
BR-12In a book that also holds carry-forward legs, an intraday leg shows Squared off after the auto square-off, valued at the square-off time at the hovered level (Assumption). Its value and P&L show greyed out, as disabled, with no range, and its P&L still counts in the totalS3

BR-15The future zone shows trading hours only (9:15 am to 3:30 pm on trading days), with a separator at each new day, as past candles do (Assumption). Open item: how decay over nights, weekends and holidays is counted (the research counted trading minutes only: 375 a session, 252 sessions a year)S2, S3
BR-16In the future zone, the hovered time snaps to the index chart's candle interval and the level to the nearest 5 index points (Assumption). BR-20Later release: a target or stop from a pin is offered only on a held single position (S2). The projected premium against the current price decides which one, not profit or loss: for a bought option, above the current price is a target and below is a stop; for a sold option, the reverse. Neither is offered within one tick of the current price. BR-23Hold-to-peek: holding ⌥ Option (Mac) or Alt (Windows) shows Payoff (S2 or S3 by position count) and letting go hides it. The peek starts after the key has been held alone for about 140 ms (Assumption), so a shortcut that starts with the same key (⌘ ⌥ 1 for 1-Tap, Alt+Tab) runs as before and doesn't open Payoff. While the key is held, the arrow keys move the point and Enter pins it; any other key ends the peek. The peek ends when the window loses focus, and doesn't start while focus is in a text input or a confirm modal (M1) is open. Clicking the Payoff button keeps Payoff on until it is clicked again or Esc is pressed. In the Later release, a pin placed while peeking keeps Payoff on after the key is let go, until the pin is removed. BR-24Turning Payoff on, by the button, the peek key or N1, doesn't move or rescale the time axis of either chart: the view the trader had stays, and the future zone shows in whatever space lies right of Now. The charts can then scroll as far as BR-27 allows; past the axis end (BR-03) the chart is disabled (BR-26). A horizon chip fits the charts from now to its time; none is picked on open, and dragging or zooming the index chart clears the pick. Turning Payoff off returns each chart to the view it had before. The price scale may shift a little to make room for the Payoff header (Assumption). Open item: whether the future zone needs a minimum width on open (today it is the chart's right margin)S2, S3
BR-25The index chart and the option chart each keep their own timeframe, e.g. Nifty on 3m and the option on 5m, and scroll and zoom on their own, with Payoff on or off. A new timeframe keeps the candle width: the window grows or shrinks with the candle size, anchored at its right edge; with a range picked (1D, 5D, 1M) the window stays on that range. Candles start at 9:15 am each session, so 2m, 10m, 30m and 1h candles never run across the night, and the last one of a day can be shorter. A horizon chip moves both charts to the same window; after that each moves on its own. The hovered point snaps to the index chart's candles, and the option chart shows its marker only when that time is in its viewS1, S2, S3
BR-26Past the axis end (BR-03), the index chart, and in S2 the option chart, show a disabled area: diagonal stripes tied to the time axis, a thin edge line at the axis end, and a centred label, After expiry, After auto square-off for an intraday-only book, or Expired when every position has already expired (then the area starts at Now). Inside it there is no shading, crosshair, readout point or pin, and the readout shows Now values. It applies to every position, bought or sold, one or several, and to pre-tradeS2, S3
BR-27Both charts drag left back to the first candle loaded and right into empty space up to 3:30 pm on the next trading day, with Payoff on or off. With Payoff on, they drag as far as the axis end when that is later. With Payoff off, the space right of Now is emptyS1, S2, S3
BR-28Mirrored crosshair. Hovering one chart draws a second crosshair on the other at the same minute, not snapped to either chart's candles, so it holds whatever each timeframe; the candle that contains that minute is highlighted. Its level matches the hovered one: up to Now, the option's actual price at that minute, moved by what the model says the option gains or loses between Nifty's actual level then and the hovered level (on Nifty's actual level it is the option's actual price); at Now, the live prices; after Now with Payoff on, the Payoff value at the snapped point (BR-16), so it passes through the option chart's marker. 12. Release Plan

MVP (first design pass and first release)

  • Surfaces: S1 (changed), N1, S2, S3, N2.
  • Requirements: FR-01 to FR-13, FR-15 to FR-19, FR-23 to FR-30; NFR-01 to NFR-08.

Later

  • Surfaces: D1, M1, N3, and the scheduled exit marker.
  • Requirements: FR-20 to FR-22.
  • Needs Orders to build exit at a time (BR-21) and to confirm BR-20 and BR-22.

Waiting for confirmation before build

  • Pricing model (BR-08), range method (BR-09), freshness threshold (BR-18), auto square-off time (BR-04), decay over nights and weekends (BR-15), single futures layout (BR-02), compliance wording (Model label, order notes), Windows availability of the Sahi Desktop app (NFR-08), the hold-to-peek key on Windows browsers (BR-23), the future zone's width on open (BR-24), how much history the charts load for dragging left (BR-27).

Launch criteria

  1. Accuracy: on a back-test of past expiry days, the range covers at least the target share of outcomes (NFR-02).
  2. Performance: NFR-01 holds on a 10-leg book in Chrome and in the Sahi Desktop app, on a reference machine. Open item: reference machine.
  3. Dates: expiry markers match the exchange contract list for every contract listed at launch, holiday shifts included.
  4. Compliance sign-off on all copy: Model label, readout, N1, N2.
  5. Usability: 8 to 10 expiry-day buyers complete the research's prototype tasks (find the breakeven at 12:00, decide whether to hold or exit at 1:30, read a weekly plus monthly book). Open item: pass bar.
  6. Analytics events in each build sheet fire correctly.
  7. Keyboard and screen reader checks pass at the target level (NFR-04).

Rollout

  • Behind a feature flag: internal users, then a beta for a share of active desktop Scalper users, then all desktop users. Open item: beta share and length.
  • The flag can turn Payoff off without a release (Assumption).
  • Mobile follows with its own PRD.

Measurement after launch

  • Track the 1.1 metrics for Payoff users against similar traders who don't use it. Open item: comparison method and review cadence.
  • Watch the guardrails (trades per trader per day, expiry-day buys of far out-of-the-money strikes); a breach pauses the rollout (Assumption).
  • Check the range every week: the share of outcomes that landed inside it (NFR-02).
