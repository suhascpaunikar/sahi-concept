Quick Action Wheel

Sahi Option Scalper · PRD v1.4 prototype · mock data

Hold a timeframe button for 300 ms, slide onto a sector, release. Letting go on the button, in a gap or past the ring cancels.

Tap it for the chart menu. When the chart is too small for the wheel, holding shows Release here for Quick actions.

Keyboard: Enter opens the menu; Shift+F10 or Q opens Quick actions.
